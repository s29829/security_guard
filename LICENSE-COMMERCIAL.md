## Commercial use (written permission needed)

You need a separate written license from the author if you use the plugin for any commercial
purpose, for example:

- in a company or another for-profit organization (also for internal use),
- to provide services to clients (installation, hosting, configuration, support),
- in a paid product or service, or by selling or redistributing the plugin for profit.

To request a commercial license, contact the author through the GitHub profile
<https://github.com/s29829>. State the company, the plugin and the intended use.
